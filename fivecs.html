What this can and cannot tell you

  • It reads wording. A plan can name every strand and still teach none of them, and a strong lesson can score badly because you write in shorthand.
  • Cognition is the easiest strand to fake on paper and the hardest to see in a plan. Ask who is doing the reasoning, not which verbs appear.
  • Community and culture usually live in the questions you ask on the day, so a plan rarely records them even when the lesson has them.
  • A planning aid, not an approved lesson. You remain the judge of accuracy, policy and cultural fit.
