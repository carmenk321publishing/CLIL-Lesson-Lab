Every package contains

  1. 01

    Where this lesson sits

    The bridge back, including whatever you said went wrong last time.

  2. 02

    Objectives

    Content tagged to a Bloom level suited to the age band. Language split into of, for and through.

  3. 03

    Pre-task scaffold

    What goes in place before the content arrives, with text load matched to your learners.

  4. 04

    Input and main task

    Group work, or genuinely rebuilt around an information gap for one to one.

  5. 05

    Report and look ahead

    Content and language evidence gathered separately. Not the same list.

  6. 06

    Questions for a colleague

    Subject accuracy, policy, culture, access. Flagged, not answered.

It covers the side you did not train in

Language teachers get subject accuracy prompts and the misconceptions to watch for. Subject teachers get the language demand of the task made visible, with the frames learners will need. Teach both and you get a balance check instead.

Sourced, then audited, then flagged

Subject content is written from retrieved sources, never from memory, and every claim carries a citation. A second pass then audits it and reports what it cannot confirm, including its own mistakes. Accuracy, policy and cultural fit remain yours to judge. Structure follows public frameworks: CEFR, Council of Europe and the European Framework for CLIL Teacher Education.
